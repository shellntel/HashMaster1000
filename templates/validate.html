Important: Review Before Excluding Lines

Some lines in your pwdump file have issues that require your attention. Before excluding any lines, please consider:

  • Lines with FATAL errors cannot be processed and are automatically excluded.
  • Lines with warnings can still be processed. Unchecking them will exclude those accounts from analysis.
  • If you exclude valid accounts, your statistics will be inaccurate. For example, if an account appears corrupt but is actually valid, excluding it means that account won't be counted in your password analysis results.
  • Recommendation: Only exclude lines that you are certain are invalid or should not be analyzed.
